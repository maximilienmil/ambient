# Latent interface direction

Confirmed by the owner, October 20, 2026.

## Visual reference

Use the warm Horizon v2 review as the sole visual reference. Its yellow/lilac
landscape and sun, warm paper, dark olive capacity surface, lilac advisor, and
glass range/goal controls define the product. The older iOS and midnight demos
remain historical references, not additional visual directions to combine.

This is an everyday task interface for a Bee owner. A person should understand
the choice without knowing what a digital twin, model distribution or fork is.

## The decision flow

- Start with the actual conflict: the stated goal, time needed, and time left.
- Compare two named actions with visible consequences. For the sample week:
  ask to move presentation work to Monday and keep Friday for the release;
  or help by Thursday and finish release work Monday.
- Keep the smaller-release option secondary, with its scope consequence visible.
- Selecting a choice updates concrete focus times and the proposed next action.
- The phone view places the A/B paths on one weekday timeline, with labeled
  release/presentation blocks. Selecting a path there selects the same choice
  as the cards above and updates the plan below. C stays available as a
  secondary choice.
- The dark olive panel shows hours and actual proposed blocks. Do not replace
  those facts with an unlabeled curve, abstract multiverse or personal score.
- The lilac panel explains the recommendation and offers review of the message
  and focus time. Its button says what opens next.
- Review uses ordinary email, date and time fields. The final control names the
  real effect: save a local draft/holds, or send a request/reserve Google time.
- A proposed request remains conditional on the other person's agreement.
  Calendar bookings and sending require real provider receipts.

## Materials and language

Glass belongs on the small range and goal controls above the illustrated field.
Content remains on stable readable surfaces. The original landscape is static;
motion accompanies selection or result changes. Respect reduced motion.

Use one readable sans family, a short type scale, clear selected/focus/disabled
states, consistent library icons, and sufficient touch targets. Keep the warm
palette the owner chose. Avoid adding ornamental icon tiles, glow, dashboards,
confidence badges or extra metric cards.

Copy names a task, source, consequence or action. Remove repeated slogans,
dramatic fragments, model jargon, and explanations that restate visible labels.
Evidence and model configuration stay behind optional details. Unknown outcomes
remain explicit at the point where they affect a decision.

Grow is a quiet editable goal list with supporting observations and source
details. Reconcile compares possible duplicate records inline and offers a
reversible local merge. Neither needs another dashboard hero.

The mobile page is responsive web. There is no native Swift/iOS app yet; the
older `/legacy` screen is a preserved web prototype, not a native implementation.

## Research applied

- [Impeccable](https://github.com/pbakaus/impeccable): inspected its current
  Operate, Clarify and craft guidance. Applied task hierarchy, meaningful
  controls, state clarity and bounded visual inspection. Its launcher was not
  installed or run; this is not a claimed detector audit.
- [Humanizer](https://github.com/blader/humanizer): inspected its skill and
  applied direct wording and removal of repetitive slogans without adding facts.
- [unslop](https://github.com/mshumer/unslop): inspected its empirical workflow.
  Its multi-sample Claude/Playwright generation pipeline was not run; do not
  claim a generated anti-pattern profile or score.
- Luna reviewed the saved v2 reference and live Horizon/Grow/Reconcile layouts.
  Its findings informed clearer consequences, action labels and evidence rows.

Owner choices override generic advice that would remove the approved palette,
illustration or glass. The aim is a coherent, understandable Bee companion.
