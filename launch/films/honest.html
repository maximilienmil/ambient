An agent that acts for you
has to be honest with you.

01

Other people’s words stay theirs.

Bystander speech is dropped before anything is stored. An “I’ll do it” from someone else never becomes your to-do.

02

Two models. If they disagree, it asks.

Jev and Liquid d1, a System 1 model, each weigh in. Ambient acts only when both agree.

03

A request is not a yes.

When the hive asks Jordan to move the deck, it says so. Jordan still has to agree.

04

Every action has a receipt and an undo.

Approvals are bound to a hash of exactly what will run. Sandbox by default; nothing leaves your machine until you connect an account.

05

Bee is never changed behind your back.

Duplicates merge locally, with undo. Facts Bee isn’t sure about wait for you.

Bee remembers.
Ambient makes room.
