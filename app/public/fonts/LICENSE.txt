The .woff2 files here are a subset-and-repack of the Fonetika trial cut that
was supplied for this build. The font is licensed Personal Use Only:
https://befonts.com/fonetika-font-family.html

Replace these files with a licensed cut before this app is distributed.
Each trial face also carries only 133 glyphs: the letters, the digits and
" !&,-.?@" — no percent sign, no colon, no parentheses, no curly quotes.
Anything set in Fonetika must avoid those characters or the browser will
substitute them from the fallback face.
